Daily non-negotiables

  • Hit your protein. Single most important number. Everything else flexes.
  • Sleep 8 hours. Phone out the bedroom by 10:30pm.
  • 10-min APT homework. Couch stretch, 90/90, glute bridge, dead bug, cat-cow.
  • Hit your steps. Walking calls count. Walk after dinner.
  • Log every set. If it's not tracked, it didn't happen.

Eating out — survival rules

  • Eat protein first. Always order a clear protein source.
  • Skip the bread basket. Free calories you didn't plan for.
  • Sauces on the side. Saves 200–400 kcal.
  • Pre-eat 20g whey 30 min before going out. Kills cravings.
  • Default safe orders: grilled chicken/fish + rice + veg, sushi (no tempura), Greek meze + grilled meats, steak + salad.

When to deviate

  • Back pain returns: Drop trap-bar, replace with hip thrust + back ext. Don't push through.
  • Strength drops 2+ weeks: Under-eating or under-sleeping. Check both.
  • Weight stalls 3 weeks: Drop 100 kcal/day OR add 1,000 steps/day. Not both.
  • Constantly hungry: More protein + more veg volume. Hunger ≠ low calories.
  • Dread going to gym: Take 4 days off. Plan still works — you don't.
